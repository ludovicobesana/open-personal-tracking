# TV Time GDPR CSV import

The TV Time importer runs entirely in the browser. It reads only CSV files the
person selects and does not use TV Time credentials, an API, scraping, or a
network request.

## Supported export

The supported source is the ZIP GDPR export currently produced by TV Time. The
app extracts its CSV files locally. Selecting one or more extracted CSV files
is also supported. Do not select an archive generated by open-personal-tracking.

Required: at least one of:

- `user_tv_show_data.csv`
- `followed_tv_show.csv`
- `tracking-prod-records.csv`
- `tracking-prod-records-v2.csv`

Optional supported tables:

- `seen_episode_latest.csv`
- `watched_on_episode.csv`
- `rewatched_episode.csv`
- `ratings-*-episode_votes.csv`

## Mapping and limitations

- Series title, TV Time show ID, followed/archived flags, and watched-episode
  count are imported when the corresponding columns are present.
- Movies from tracking records are imported with their title and completed
  state when a watch count is available.
- Rewatch totals are retained as an item attribute. Per-event rewatch dates
  cannot be reconstructed from the supported tables.
- When `watched_on_episode.csv`, `seen_episode_latest.csv`, or
  `rewatched_episode.csv` contains season and episode numbers, watched episodes
  are imported under generated season and episode entries. Episode titles are
  not in these tables, so entries are labelled by number (for example,
  `Season 1` and `Episode 3`).
- `tracking-prod-records-v2.csv` and `tracking-prod-records.csv` are also
  parsed for series rows. When their season, episode, and per-episode watch
  fields are available, they supply both watched and unwatched episode
  structure for a series.
- Latest episode-watch and rewatch rows are added to local history when their
  show can be matched. They represent only the event rows present in the
  export, not a complete historical timeline.
- If no granular episode rows are selected, only the source watched-episode
  count is imported; no seasons or episodes can be reconstructed.
- Lists, comments, recommendations, social data, images, and notification data
  are not imported.
- Rating vote keys are intentionally not converted: the export does not define
  their numeric scale. The preview reports this limitation.

## Safety and conflicts

The app parses and validates the complete candidate archive before it writes
anything to browser storage. The preview identifies duplicates by TV Time ID,
then by matching title and item type. The person must choose whether to skip a
duplicate or update its TV Time-derived tracking fields; an update retains
existing local notes, tags, collections, rating, and other local attributes.

Invalid, malformed, or unsupported files never replace or alter the current
archive.
